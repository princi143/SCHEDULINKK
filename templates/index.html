One gap, felt from both sides of the counter

If you're the customer

  • You wait in a queue with no idea how long it will take.
  • You call again and again just to ask “are you free?”
  • You travel there, then find out the next slot is hours away.

With Schedulink you see open slots near you, book one, and watch your place in the queue before you leave home.

If you run the business

  • Walk-ins, cancellations and no-shows wreck the plan by noon.
  • One team member is overloaded while another has nothing booked.
  • You can't tell which hours need more people and which need fewer.

With Schedulink every booking and walk-in lands in one queue, work is spread across your team, and you see your real busy hours.
